Frequently asked questions

Why is a store price different at checkout?

Retailers can change prices, availability, fees, and promotions without notice. Location and account-specific offers can also differ. Always confirm the final amount with the retailer.

How does CompareZ decide “Best Value”?

CompareZ uses the detected price and quantity to estimate a comparable unit value. If quantity cannot be detected reliably, the result is shown without a unit comparison.

Does CompareZ sell or deliver products?

No. CompareZ helps you compare. Purchases, delivery, refunds, and product support remain with the retailer you choose.

Why should I connect a retailer?

A connection can help CompareZ retrieve results appropriate to your area or retailer session. You control which retailers are connected and can disconnect them in the app.

What should I include when reporting a problem?

Include the product name, retailer, approximate time, app version, and a screenshot if possible. Never send your password, payment information, or retailer session details.

Response time

We aim to review support and privacy messages within seven business days. Account deletion requests may require identity verification.
