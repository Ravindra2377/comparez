Questions, answered

Clear before you install.

CompareZ is a comparison layer. The retailer remains the place where you complete the purchase.

01Does CompareZ sell or deliver products?

No. CompareZ helps you discover and compare available listings. Checkout, payment, fulfilment, returns, and delivery remain with the retailer you choose.

02How fresh are the prices?

CompareZ retrieves available results when you search. Retailer prices, fees, stock, and delivery terms can change, so always confirm the final details in the retailer app before paying.

03Why does quantity matter?

A lower pack price is not always better value. CompareZ uses comparable unit information when it is available so that a 500 g pack is not treated like a 1 kg pack.

04Will more stores be supported?

The experience is designed around connected services rather than a fixed platform count. New retailer connections can be added without changing how you search.

05What does BEANS do?

BEANS is CompareZ’s shopping companion. It can understand natural shopping requests, prepare ingredient lists, and guide you toward a comparison. You still review and choose the final result.

06How does CompareZ protect my account?

CompareZ uses authenticated API access, HTTPS, password hashing, request controls, and restricted service access. Never send passwords, payment details, or retailer tokens through support or BEANS.
